# Generated character and office references

Generated 5 November 2026 with GPT's built-in image generation tool (no API/CLI fallback). Eight original 16-person atlases provide 128 unique reference cells. There are 12 women and four men per atlas; the roster distributes these as three women and one man per specialty. The first atlas's interleaved gender arrangement is explicitly mapped in identities.js. All people are fictional. Portraits are art references, not dynamic appearance previews or generated 3D meshes.

Project assets: `public/office/cast-1.webp` through `cast-8.webp`, and `public/office/office-concept.webp`. Full-resolution original PNGs are copied under ignored `.private/`; generated originals also remain in Codex's generated_images folder. WebP conversion optimizes delivery without changing the illustrated content. A contact sheet was used for visual inspection. No real prospect data was supplied to image generation.
